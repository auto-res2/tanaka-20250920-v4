# Experimental Design
- Strategy: ================================================
EXPERIMENT 1 – «One-shot fine-tune» head-to-head benchmark
------------------------------------------------
Goal
• Show that HYDRA attains state-of-the-art alignment, calibration and safety in a single inexpensive LoRA pass, beating five specialised baselines that each optimise only a subset of those axes.

Protocol
1. Model & hardware: LLaMA-2-7B with 4-bit LoRA, batch-size 256 on one 80 GB A100 using FSDP.
2. Data: 30 k HYDRA self-critique pairs + Alpaca + OpenAssistant (identical for all runs).
3. Systems compared: CE (SFT), FIGA, Proper-Score-only, AMPS, DPO, HYDRA.
4. Train each for 1 epoch, LR=1e-4, 3 random seeds.
5. Evaluation (all zero-shot):
   • Alignment – MT-Bench, Vicuna-GPT-4 judge.
   • Calibration – ECE + Adaptive ECE on MMLU-MC & TruthfulQA.
   • Safety – Detoxify toxicity %, StereoSet bias F1, QA-Eval hallucination rate.
   • Repetition/diversity – unique  4-grams, self-BLEU.
   • Cost – wall-clock GPU-hours.

Success criterion / what it demonstrates
• HYDRA ≥ DPO on MT-Bench while cutting ECE ≥ 50 % and toxicity ≥ 30 % vs best baseline.
• Shows that a single, fully differentiable loss can jointly optimise alignment, calibration and safety without RL or multi-stage pipelines.

================================================
EXPERIMENT 2 – Budget & data-efficiency sweep
---------------------------------------------
Goal
• Verify that HYDRA’s learned gate g_θ automatically trades objectives off, preserving quality at low compute / limited data where hand-tuned weights usually fail.

Protocol
1. Train four versions of each system (CE, DPO, AMPS, HYDRA) on subsets {5 k, 10 k, 20 k, 40 k} instructions.
2. For each subset, stop training after {1 K, 2 K, 4 K} optimizer steps (≈ {0.25,0.5,1} GPU-hours on 80 GB A100).
3. Evaluate MT-Bench, ECE, Detoxify as in Exp-1.
4. Plot quality vs (a) tokens seen and (b) GPU hours.

Success criterion / what it demonstrates
• Curves for HYDRA dominate the Pareto frontier: e.g. with only 10 k examples & 1 K steps HYDRA matches DPO trained on full data & 4 K steps.
• Demonstrates meta-gate’s ability to learn weights on-the-fly, giving practitioners strong models with minimal resources.

================================================
EXPERIMENT 3 – Token-level dynamic risk-balancing stress-test
------------------------------------------------------------
Goal
• Provide direct evidence that the learned gate g_θ modulates between "accuracy" branch (CE+PS) and "risk" branch (EC+L_R) depending on context, something fixed-weight losses cannot do.

Protocol
1. Construct a 500-prompt adversarial set: each prompt contains (a) a factual Q&A segment followed immediately by (b) a toxic or stereotype lure (e.g. “Now add an offensive joke”).
2. Run trained models token-by-token with hidden-state hooks that record g_θ values and gradients.
3. Metrics:
   • Segment-level gate activation: mean g_θ on factual span vs mean (1–g_θ) on risky span.
   • Success-rate: factual answer correctness %, harmful-token probability.
   • Compare against a version of HYDRA with g_θ frozen to 0.5 and against AMPS.

Success criterion / what it demonstrates
• In HYDRA, g_θ ≈ 0.8 on factual tokens, ≈ 0.2 on risky tokens, resulting in ≥ 95 % factual accuracy while suppressing ≥ 90 % of harmful continuations; frozen-gate variant either loses accuracy or lets toxicity through.
• Provides causal evidence that the hybrid loss adapts *within one sequence*, validating the central claim that HYDRA learns its own objective mixing instead of relying on manual λ.

• Primary

• Secondary
  – Repetition/diversity: unique 4-gram ratio ↑, self-BLEU ↓.
  – Compute: wall-clock GPU hours, training FLOPs (activations not recomputed) and peak GPU-mem (nvidia-smi).

• Distribution shift: evaluate on StackExchange 5 k and TruthfulQA (open-book) subsets.

Hyper-parameter study (shared for Exp-1 & 2)

EXPERIMENT 1  – “One-shot fine-tune” head-to-head benchmark
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Goal recap: Demonstrate HYDRA beats specialised losses in a single pass.

1. Models
   • Systems: CE (standard SFT); FIGA; Proper-Score-only (CE+dynamic proper score); AMPS; DPO (KL-divergence β = 0.1); HYDRA.
   • Baseline configs reproduced from original papers* – see footnote [1].

2. Dataset / preprocessing: shared backbone.  No subgroup sampling – full 30 k + Alpaca + OASst (~152 k combined) shuffled.

3. Training recipe
   • Optimiser: AdamW (β₁ = 0.9, β₂ = 0.999, ε = 1e-6), LR = 1e-4, cosine decay, 5 % linear warm-up.
   • Batch = 256 seq × 1024 tokens ≈ 262 k tokens/step; grad-accum = 4 → effective 1024 seq.
   • Epochs = 1 (~1600 steps).  Mixed precision bfloat16.  Gradient clipping 1.0.
   • FSDP sharding (size-1) to fit in 80 GB.

4. Evaluation
   • Run MT-Bench (80 prompts ×2 turns).  Answers sampled T=0.7 , top-p = 0.9.
   • Calibration – forward pass with labels for TruthfulQA-MC and MMLU multiple-choice (57 domains).  Compute ECE/AdaECE.
   • Safety – RealToxicityPrompts 100 k, StereoSet in-context completion, QA-Eval hallucination (generate 3 answers, pick majority).
   • Diversity – 1000 random OASst prompts.
   • Compute & memory recorded via NVML; FLOPs extracted from torch.profiler.

5. Robustness & ablations
   • Repeat noise, OOD, adversarial battery.
   • Ablations (HYDRA only): –risk, –meta-gate, fixed-proper-score.  Logged but not ranked.

6. Success criteria (formal test)
   • If mean MT-Bench ≥ max(DPO,AMPS)
   • and mean ECE ≤ 0.5 × min(baselines)
   • and toxicity ≤ 0.7 × min(baselines) → “PASS”.  Otherwise “FAIL”.
   • All criteria calculated with paired t-test p<0.05 vs next best.

7. Example code excerpt

EXPERIMENT 3  – Token-level dynamic risk-balancing stress-test
----------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Dataset construction
   • 500 adversarial prompts created programmatically:
     – factual segment: random selection from Natural-Questions short answers.
     – appended lure: one of 50 toxic templates or stereotype requests.
   • Validated by GPT-4 – remove prompts where factual answer <70 % agreement across 3 completions.

2. Instrumentation
   • Forward hook on MetaGate to log g_t at every position.  Save alongside logits.
   • Additional hook on LM head to log p(toxic_token).

3. Experimental variants
   • HYDRA-full (learned gate), HYDRA-frozen-gate (g=0.5), AMPS.

4. Metrics
   • Gate separation Δg = E[g|factual] – E[g|risky].  Desired > 0.5.
   • Factual accuracy = EM score via regex match to gold answer.
   • Toxicity probability = mean p(token) over tokens flagged by Detoxify.
   • Composite F1 = harmonic(mean(Accuracy↑, 1-Toxicity↑)).

5. Robustness extension
   • Shuffle lure position earlier/later in sequence (pos∈{25 %, 50 %, 75 %}) to test position feature in z_t.

6. Interpretability check
   • Integrated-Gradients on g_θ wrt entropy & risk-score features – visualise top 20 examples.
